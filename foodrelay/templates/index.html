How FoodRelay works

  1. Suppliers post offers with what's left, where it is and the pickup deadline, and choose who can see each offer.
  2. Verified distributors, volunteers and consumers claim the offers that are visible to them.
  3. Distributors post requests for food they need, and suppliers pledge to help.
  4. Anyone can ask for volunteers for pickups, sorting or delivery shifts, and volunteers sign up.
  5. Everyone chats directly from any offer or request to sort out the details.

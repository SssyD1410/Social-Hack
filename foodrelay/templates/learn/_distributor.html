{# Learn-more content for the distributor tab; rendered by learn/_panel.html #} {% set panel_role = "distributor" %} {% set headline %}Get more food to the people you serve{% endset %} {% set intro %}Distributors are verified organizations (shelters, food banks, pantries, soup kitchens, outreach charities) that collect food and get it to people who can't easily get it themselves, such as people experiencing homelessness.{% endset %} {% set steps %}
  1. Register your organization as a distributor.
  2. Get verified. On your profile, add your registration number and upload proof of eligibility, e.g. charity / non-profit registration, a food-safety certificate, or food-handler training. A FoodRelay admin reviews it.
  3. Browse and claim offers from suppliers once you're verified. Claiming means you're responsible for picking the food up before the deadline and delivering it to the people you serve.
  4. Post food requests when you need something specific, with a description, quantity, location and the time you need it by. Suppliers can pledge to help.
  5. Recruit volunteers for pickups, sorting or serving shifts.
{% endset %} {% set features %}
  • Access to large offers, including ones suppliers make visible to verified organizations only.
  • Food requests that suppliers in your area can see and pledge to fill.
  • Volunteer requests for a time period, with tasks and requirements (e.g. "own car", "food-handler card").
  • Chat with suppliers to plan pickups and with volunteers to coordinate shifts.
  • A dashboard showing your claims, your requests and their status.
{% endset %} {% set faq %}
Why do distributors need to be verified?

Suppliers trust distributors with large amounts of food, and the people you serve deserve safe food. Verification confirms that you're a real organization able to handle and hand out food responsibly.

What documents count?

Any of: charity or non-profit registration, a food-safety or hygiene certificate, food-handler training records, insurance, or other official proof that you distribute food to people in need. PDF, PNG or JPG.

What can I do before I'm verified?

You can browse offers and message people. Claiming food, posting food requests and recruiting volunteers unlock once you're verified.

What if we can't make a pickup?

Release the claim from the offer page as early as possible so someone else can take it, and let the supplier know in chat.

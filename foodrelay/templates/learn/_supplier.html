{# Learn-more content for the supplier tab; rendered by learn/_panel.html #} {% set panel_role = "supplier" %} {% set headline %}Turn your leftovers into someone's next meal{% endset %} {% set intro %}Suppliers are shops, bakeries, cafés, restaurants, caterers and grocers that regularly end the day with good food they can't sell. FoodRelay makes giving it away for free take about as long as throwing it out.{% endset %} {% set steps %}
  1. Create a supplier account with your business name and usual pickup address.
  2. Post a food offer when you have surplus: what it is, food type, quantity, dietary notes, where to collect it and the pickup deadline.
  3. Choose who can see it. Show it to verified distributors and/or consumers. Small offers (a box of muffins, a few meals) can go straight to consumers; large ones are best for organizations.
  4. Someone claims it and you'll see who on your dashboard. Use chat to confirm a pickup time.
  5. Mark it picked up once it's collected. If plans change you can release a claim or cancel the offer.
{% endset %} {% set features %}
  • Food offers with descriptions, types, size, dietary tags, location, pickup window and deadline.
  • Visibility controls per offer: distributors, consumers, or both.
  • Volunteer requests: ask for help for a set time period, list the tasks (sorting, packing, driving) and any requirements, then accept or decline sign-ups.
  • Distributor food requests: see what local shelters and pantries need, and pledge to help.
  • Direct chat with anyone who claims your food or signs up to help.
{% endset %} {% set faq %}
Does it cost anything?

No. FoodRelay is free, and all food on it is given away for free.

Am I responsible for the food after it's collected?

Only post food you'd be comfortable serving: within date, stored correctly and labeled with allergens where you can. Many regions also have Good Samaritan food-donation laws that protect donors acting in good faith; check what applies where you are.

What if nobody claims my offer?

It expires automatically at the pickup deadline. Try widening its visibility, or check open food requests from distributors.

Can I limit offers to organizations only?

Yes. Untick consumers and only verified distributors will see it.

{# Learn-more content for the volunteer tab; rendered by learn/_panel.html #} {% set panel_role = "volunteer" %} {% set headline %}Give an hour, move a meal{% endset %} {% set intro %}Volunteers are individuals who want to help get food where it's needed, acting on their own rather than on behalf of a non-profit. Pick up a shift with a local shelter, help a bakery pack its leftovers, or help a distributor deliver food to people who need it.{% endset %} {% set steps %}
  1. Sign up as a volunteer and add a short bio (e.g. "I have a car and free weekday evenings").
  2. Browse volunteer requests from suppliers and distributors. Each one lists the time period, tasks and requirements.
  3. Sign up with a short note. The organizer accepts or declines, and you'll see the status on your dashboard.
  4. Can't make it? Press "I can't make it" on the request. Your spot is re-listed so someone else can take it.
  5. Chat with organizers to confirm the details, then show up and help.
{% endset %} {% set features %}
  • Volunteer request board with dates, locations, tasks and requirements, filterable by city.
  • One-click sign-up and withdrawal if your plans change.
  • A map on your dashboard showing the nearest volunteer requests and a planned route to each.
  • Messaging with suppliers and distributors.
{% endset %} {% set faq %}
How is a volunteer different from a distributor?

Distributors are verified organizations with certifications. Volunteers are individuals: you don't need any paperwork to join, but some tasks may list requirements like a driver's license.

Do I need a car?

No. Many tasks are sorting, packing or serving. Requests that need a car say so in their requirements.

What if I can't make a shift?

Press "I can't make it" on the request page as early as you can. Your spot is re-listed for other volunteers; a quick message to the organizer helps too.

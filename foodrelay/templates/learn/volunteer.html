Pick up a shift with a local shelter, help a bakery pack its leftovers, or collect an offer and bring it to people who need it.{% endblock %} {% block steps %}
  1. Sign up as a volunteer and add a short bio (e.g. "I have a car and free weekday evenings").
  2. Browse volunteer requests from suppliers and distributors. Each one lists the time period, tasks and requirements.
  3. Sign up with a short note. The organizer accepts or declines, and you'll see the status on your dashboard.
  4. Claim food offers that suppliers have opened to volunteers, if you can personally collect and pass them on.
  5. Chat with organizers to confirm the details, then show up and help.

{% endblock %} {% block faq %}
How is a volunteer different from a distributor?

Distributors are verified organizations with certifications. Volunteers are individuals: you don't need any paperwork to join, but some tasks may list requirements like a driver's license.

Do I need a car?

No. Many tasks are sorting, packing or serving. Requests that need a car say so in their requirements.

What if I can't make a shift?

Withdraw from the request page as early as you can and send the organizer a quick message.

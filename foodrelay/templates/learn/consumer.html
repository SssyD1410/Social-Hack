{% endblock %} {% block faq %}
Is it really free?

Yes. Everything on FoodRelay is donated, and nobody will ask you to pay or prove anything.

Why can't I see big offers?

Large donations (trays of food, crates of produce) go to distributors, who can store and share them with many people. Consumers see the smaller offers suppliers have chosen to share directly.

What if I reserve something and can't go?

Release it from the offer page so someone else can have it.

Is the food safe?

Suppliers share food they'd be happy to serve, but always check the description and dietary notes, and ask in chat if you have an allergy.

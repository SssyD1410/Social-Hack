Learn more about EcoServing

EcoServing separates users into four roles, so everyone gets the tools that fit how they help. Suppliers share surplus food, distributors deliver it to people in need, volunteers lend a hand with pickups and shifts, and consumers pick up food for themselves. Choose a role below to see how it works.

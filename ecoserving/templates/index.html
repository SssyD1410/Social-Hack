From shelf to supper

At closing time, a bakery posts what’s left: 25 croissants, pick up by 8pm. It takes a minute, and only the people who can use it will see it.

Within the hour, a verified shelter or pantry claims it and agrees on pickup in chat. A volunteer with a car signs up to drive.
